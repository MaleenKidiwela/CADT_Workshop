The workshop brings together people from universities, agencies, ports,
utilities, tribal and First Nation governments, and companies, across two
countries. We expect everyone to help make it a place where each of them can
contribute.

## Expectations

- Treat every participant with respect. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Make room. Invite quieter voices in, and do not dominate a session.
- Listen to knowledge that comes in forms other than your own. Indigenous
  knowledge, operational experience, and scientific results all count.
- Respect data. Do not share data, figures, or documents beyond the room
  without the owner's agreement.
- No harassment of any kind: unwelcome comments about identity, unwanted
  attention, intimidation, or disruption.

## Reporting

If something happens that you want addressed, speak to any of the conveners, in
person or by email through the contact on the home page. Reports are handled
in confidence. Organizers may ask a participant to leave a session or the
workshop.

## Attribution

Ideas surfaced at the workshop belong to the group. Outputs will credit the
workshop and, where a contribution is clearly attributable, the contributor.
Ask before quoting a participant in public.
